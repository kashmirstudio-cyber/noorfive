About Noor 5

A small studio with a big love for colour, beauty and play.

Who we are

Noor 5 is an independent game studio focused on makeup, beauty and style games for mobile devices. Noor means light, and that's what we want our games to bring: bright, cheerful moments where players can relax and express themselves.

We're just getting started. Our first game is being prepared for Google Play, and we have many more ideas on the way.
